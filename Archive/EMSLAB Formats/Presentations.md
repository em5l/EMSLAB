## Slide Master Information

This template utilizes a **Slide Master** to ensure consistency across all slides. 

* **Global Elements:** The text "(PRESENTOR NAME)" and "(SEMINAR NAME)" appears on every slide automatically. 
* **Editing:** To change these placeholders, you must edit the Slide Master view rather than individual slides.
